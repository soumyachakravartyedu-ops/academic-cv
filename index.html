About

Dr. Soumyabrata Chakravarty is an Assistant Professor at Brainware University, Kolkata. His research focuses on sustainable manufacturing, industrial waste valorization, aluminium metal matrix composites, machinability, materials characterization, process optimization and circular-economy-oriented engineering.
