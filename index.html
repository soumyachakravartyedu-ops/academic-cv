About

Dr. Soumyabrata Chakravarty is an Assistant Professor at Brainware University, Kolkata. His research includes aluminium metal matrix composites, industrial waste reutilization, machinability and tool wear, sustainable manufacturing, materials characterization, process optimization, energy optimization and circular economy.
