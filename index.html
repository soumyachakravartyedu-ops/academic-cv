About

Dr. Soumyabrata Chakravarty is an Assistant Professor at Brainware University, Kolkata, with 5+ years of experience across academia and clean-technology R&D. His work focuses on converting industrial waste into useful engineering resources, developing sustainable composite materials, understanding machinability and tribological behaviour, and applying statistical and computational tools to engineering-process optimization.
